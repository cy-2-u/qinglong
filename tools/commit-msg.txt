fix(sched): canonicalize hourly anchors to top of hour (no minute in UI contract)

Panel exposes no minute picker, yet hourly tasks inherited the creation-
minute phase (created 09:37 -> anchors forever at :37). initialNext for
hourly now floors to the current hour and steps N hours from there;
healedAnchor extends to hourly n=1 so legacy drifted anchors self-correct
to the next full hour. Editor preview labels hourly as top-of-hour.
