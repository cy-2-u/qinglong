fix(scheduler): align DO alarm to nearest task anchor (phase-locked :xx drift)

Alarm used to re-arm as now+1h, phase-locking heartbeats to whatever minute
the DO first woke (tasks ran daily at 09:56 instead of their 09:00 anchor).
ensureAlarm now recomputes alarm = min(nearest enabled next_run_at + 5s,
now + 1h): tasks run seconds after their anchor regardless of minute; task
create/update/toggle/delete trigger a recompute; hourly watchdog semantics
unchanged. Pure decision extracted as nextAlarmAt() with 5 unit tests.
